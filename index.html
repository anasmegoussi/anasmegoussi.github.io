01 / Fintech tool · 2026

Portfolio Analyzer

Built alone · Thesis artefact

The fund I worked at tracked every transaction by hand and recalculated positions in Excel before each investment committee. I built a web app that does it automatically: drop the transaction file, get every position, valuation and realised P&L in seconds.

  • Ran on the fund’s full history: more than 1,600 transactions and 36 open positions across several currencies and countries.
  • Two costing engines, FIFO and weighted average cost (CUMP), checked by automated tests.
  • Reads Excel files with French or English column names and detects the layout on its own.
  • Dashboard by company and country, positions table, exports and user accounts.

Research

Thesis

Bachelor’s thesis, IMT Business School, 2026. Defended and graded 17/20.

Research question

How do automation and AI contribute to operational portfolio management in an alternative investment fund focused on African markets?

A single case study of a small Luxembourg-based fund investing in African growth companies, where one portfolio manager tracked orders, average costs and country exposure by hand in Excel. The thesis builds and evaluates a tool for that exact job.

17/20final grade
Read the thesis (PDF) ↗

English translation, anonymised: the fund and its staff are not named and confidential data has been removed.

Method: design science research

  1. Define the problem from daily work at the fund.
  2. Review the literature on AI in asset management and data quality in emerging markets.
  3. Build the artefact: Portfolio Analyzer.
  4. Evaluate it against the previous Excel process.

Key references

  • Hevner et al. (2004), MIS Quarterly
  • Peffers et al. (2007)
  • Gregor & Hevner (2013)
  • Sutiene et al. (2024), Frontiers in AI
